Webster Hall · Selected Venues

Live music at Webster Hall.

Solo violin, jazz piano, and DJ + live violin at the historic East Village venue — corporate parties, brand launches, private events, and full-production evenings across the Grand Ballroom, Marlin Room, and Studio spaces.

A room built for stage.

Webster Hall is the only spot on Julie's selected-venues list that reads as a proper production stage rather than a hotel ballroom or a private club room. The Grand Ballroom holds a full lighting rig, a real stage, and the kind of PA that a corporate brand event or product launch wants when the reveal needs a room-filling moment. That matters for the format Julie brings — original score performed live to a video reveal, or hybrid DJ + live violin at reception volume where the sound system can actually keep up.

Formats that fit Webster Hall.

Brand launches and product reveals — Original score composed to a client's launch video, performed live to the screen behind Julie on the Grand Ballroom stage. Cue points and run of show worked out with your production team in advance.

Corporate parties and holiday events — DJ + live violin format for the reception, real strings played over house, hip-hop, R&B, and pop. Full sound system + lighting through the venue's house AV.

Cocktail arrivals in the Marlin Room — Solo violin or jazz piano at conversation level while guests come in through the Grand Ballroom entrance.

Full-production evenings — Featured moments across the night — walk-on themes, transition music, live music under video segments. Julie works off a run of show provided by your production team.

Webster Hall logistics.

The room has professional in-house production, an experienced technical team, and everything a full-scale corporate takeover needs on stage. Julie feeds directly into house sound via wired XLR, joins a production call with the venue's AV lead before large events, and travels with a self-contained backup rig if the client is bringing outside production instead of using the house system.
