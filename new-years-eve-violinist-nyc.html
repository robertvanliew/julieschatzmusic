Live Violin for New Year's Eve in New York

Julie Schatz plays live violin and piano for New Year's Eve parties and galas in New York City and New Jersey, and performs live violin over a DJ set for the countdown and after.

It is one night, so she takes one event. Send your details for a New Year's Eve quote.

Private parties and galas

A private party at home, in a loft or in a private room usually wants two things: music people can talk over early in the night, then something to dance to. Solo violin or jazz piano covers the first part. DJ and live violin covers the second.

A gala or ticketed event has a run of show. Julie works from it with your planner or production team: music for arrivals, music through dinner, and a set timed to the countdown. She is used to working with in-house AV.

The midnight moment

The night builds to one minute, so plan the music around it. A common shape is a live violin feature as the countdown ends, then straight into the DJ and live violin set while the room is still on its feet. Tell Julie how you want midnight to feel and she will plan the cue with you.

Formats

  • Solo violin. One musician, a small footprint, and the easiest fit for arrivals and cocktail hour.
  • Jazz piano. Standards, soul and modern covers at conversation level for dinner.
  • Violin and piano duo. Julie switching between the two, or the two-musician Soul Shades duo for a fuller sound.
  • DJ and live violin. A full DJ set with violin played live over it, for when the room is ready to dance.
