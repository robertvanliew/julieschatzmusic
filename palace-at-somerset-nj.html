Live music at The Palace at Somerset Park.

Solo violin, jazz piano, and DJ + live violin at the Somerset ballroom estate — corporate holiday parties, weddings, galas, and milestone private events. Upcoming: Julie's December 2026 corporate holiday party booking here.

A ballroom estate built for scale.

The Palace at Somerset Park is one of Central New Jersey's largest ballroom-estate venues, with multiple ballroom spaces plus an atrium and grand foyer that hold receptions from 100 to 700 guests. Corporate holiday parties are one of the room's natural fits — the scale accommodates a full evening arc (cocktail arrivals → seated dinner → dancing) with production room to spare. Julie's hybrid DJ + live violin format fills the space cleanly without needing to hire a separate ensemble for each phase of the evening.

Formats that fit Palace at Somerset.

Corporate holiday parties — Solo violin for cocktail arrivals in the foyer, jazz piano through seated dinner, DJ + live violin for after-dinner dancing. One artist, one contract. Julie has a December 2026 corporate holiday party on the calendar here.

Weddings — Ceremony solo violin in the atrium or garden, jazz piano through cocktail hour, DJ + live violin for the reception. Full standard rate sheet applies (Zone 2).

Galas and benefits — Walk-on themes for honorees, live cues under video segments, musical stings for award announcements. Coordinated with the venue's house AV.

Bar and bat mitzvahs — Cocktail hour solo violin or piano, DJ + live violin for the party. Familiar with the room's mitzvah traditions.

Somerset logistics.

The Palace at Somerset Park is roughly 40 miles from Brooklyn (Zone 2). The venue has professional in-house event coordination, an experienced AV team, and multiple loading points scaled to which ballroom is booked. Julie coordinates directly with the property's event captain, joins a production call before large events, and issues COI within 48 hours naming The Palace at Somerset Park as additional insured.
