Live music built around your brand moment.

I'm a violinist, pianist, vocalist, producer and composer. I build the performance around what your event is actually for. That might be an original score written to your product video and performed live in sync with it, a set at MoMA that moves from Bach to Wu-Tang without losing the room, or unobtrusive instrumental music for a client dinner where the conversation is the point. I work with in-house AV and production teams, and I'm comfortable on a large stage with a full production behind me.
