Pendry Natirar · Selected Venues

Live music at Pendry Natirar.

Solo violin, jazz piano, and DJ + live violin at the historic Peapack estate — weddings, corporate retreats, private client dinners, and milestone events on the 500-acre Somerset County property.

A country-estate room with luxury-hotel infrastructure.

Pendry Natirar is the rare venue that reads as a private country estate and runs like a five-star hotel — original mansion rooms with fireplaces, the Great Room ballroom, an outdoor courtyard, and dedicated event spaces across the property. That mix does something specific for music: solo violin fits the historic rooms without needing amplification, then the ballroom scales up for a hybrid DJ + live violin reception where sound can actually breathe. Julie's formats move naturally across a Natirar evening from intimate arrivals to full-room dancing.

Formats that fit Pendry Natirar.

Weddings — Ceremony solo violin in the outdoor gardens or historic mansion rooms, jazz piano through cocktail hour, DJ + live violin for the reception. Zone 2 pricing (Natirar is ~40 miles from Brooklyn).

Corporate retreats and executive dinners — Solo violin during cocktail arrivals, jazz piano at conversation level through dinner. Discreet, low-key, exactly the register a private company retreat wants.

Milestone private events — Anniversary dinners, milestone birthdays, engagement parties on the estate. Both intimate rooms and full-property formats.

Brand launches and press events — Original score composed to a client's brand video and performed live during a reveal or keynote in the Great Room.

Pendry Natirar logistics.

Natirar is a Pendry (luxury hotel brand) property with full in-house event coordination and standards that match. Julie coordinates directly with the property's catering and events team, works with the in-house AV lead on run-of-show, issues COI within 48 hours, and travels light for the intimate historic rooms while bringing full backup rig for the Great Room reception spaces. Zone 2 pricing applies (Peapack is roughly 40 miles from Brooklyn).
