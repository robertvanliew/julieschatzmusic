Live music at Il Villaggio, Carlstadt.

Solo violin, jazz piano, and DJ + live violin at the Bergen County Italian-heritage venue — weddings, receptions, and milestone celebrations across Il Villaggio's three ballroom spaces.

A New Jersey ballroom institution.

Il Villaggio has been running weddings and large private receptions in Carlstadt for decades — a Bergen County institution with three distinct ballroom spaces, a signature staircase entrance, and the kind of catering the room is famous for. For music, that means an audience with real expectations: cocktail hour reads properly, the reception dance floor has to actually work for guests who have been to a hundred weddings here, and the whole evening is expected to flow between arms of the venue as guests move between spaces.

Formats that fit Il Villaggio.

Wedding cocktail hour — Solo violin or jazz piano at conversation level as guests come in through the signature staircase. Positioning coordinated with the venue's catering flow.

Reception dancing — DJ + live violin format. Full sound and lighting, real strings played over house, hip-hop, R&B, and pop. Reads properly for the Il Villaggio crowd.

Combined booking — Cocktail hour into reception — one artist, one load-in, one contract. Julie has a September 2026 wedding cocktail-and-reception booking at Il Villaggio on the calendar.

Private receptions and milestone events — Sweet sixteens, anniversaries, engagement parties. The venue's three ballrooms accommodate everything from 80-guest intimate rooms to 400-guest full ballroom.

Il Villaggio logistics.

Il Villaggio is roughly 20 miles from Brooklyn — right on the Zone 1/Zone 2 border. Julie treats it as Zone 2 for planning purposes (+$100 travel). The venue's catering and coordination team is experienced and runs the room's specific traditions (grand entrance, family-style dinner service, floor timing) well. Julie coordinates load-in with the venue's event captain and stays out of the way of the catering flow, which is meaningful at Il Villaggio.
