One Saturday. One booking.

Travel out east means one booking per day across roughly fourteen summer Saturdays. If Julie is available for your date, she is available for your date — not squeezing between two other events with a ferry in the middle. The calendar is the calendar, and it's why booking early is the whole game out here.

Discretion is the standard.

No client, host, or vessel is ever named publicly. This is a market where the referrals come through captains, chief stews, family office assistants, and the two or three planners in the Hamptons who actually run the private-estate calendar. All of them screen for who talks and who doesn't. Julie doesn't.

If your event requires an NDA before you'll even send the address, that's normal and welcome. She signs event NDAs same-day.

Also — the real reason the accommodation clause matters. An event that ends at 11pm in Bridgehampton is not something you drive home from at 1am. Making sure lodging is arranged before load-in is not a diva request; it's the difference between a musician who's alert during the reception and one who is watching the clock. This is the norm across every serious performer working the circuit.
