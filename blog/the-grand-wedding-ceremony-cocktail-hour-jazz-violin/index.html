Classy Pop at The Grand: Jazz Violin for a Ceremony and Cocktail Hour

Electric jazz violin for a ceremony and cocktail hour at The Grand. Classy pop with elegance and pizzazz from Julie Schatz Music.

On November 8 I had the pleasure of playing the ceremony and cocktail hour for a wedding at The Grand, and it was the kind of day that reminds me why I love this work.

The brief was elegant but fun. Instead of a traditional classical program, I played the pop songs the couple and their guests actually know and love, reimagined through a jazz lens on electric violin. Familiar melodies, a little swing, a little improvisation, and a sound that feels polished without ever feeling stuffy.

The ceremony

A ceremony sets the emotional tone for the whole day. I kept things warm and graceful for the guest arrival and processional, then brought the energy up for the recessional so the newlyweds walked back up the aisle to something joyful. Playing live over backing tracks gives the music a full, rich sound, even in a large space.

Cocktail hour

Cocktail hour is where the classy pop vibe really comes alive. Guests are mingling, drinks are flowing, and the music should lift the room without competing with conversation. I mixed smooth R&B, soul and pop favorites with jazzy phrasing, and it was so much fun watching guests stop mid conversation when they recognized a song.

Why jazz violin works for weddings

Live violin adds instant elegance. A jazz approach adds personality. Together they give your day a soundtrack that feels sophisticated and current at the same time, and every set can be tailored to your taste, from timeless love songs to modern hits.

Thank you to The Grand and to this wonderful couple for having me. Congratulations!
