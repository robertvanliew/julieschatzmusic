Private Client Dinners

Discreet live music for private client dinners.

Solo piano or violin at conversation level for hedge fund, family office, private equity, and law firm client dinners across NYC. NDAs signed same-day. Zero mention of clients in Julie's portfolio, on the site, or on social. This booking exists to be invisible in the best possible way.

The register that a client dinner actually needs.

The music at a private client dinner has one job: raise the room by a quarter-notch without becoming the subject of the room. Conversation stays audible, the space feels considered, and no one has to compliment the musician for the booking to be a success. The moment guests are aware of the music as a distinct thing, it has failed.

Julie plays this register natively. Solo violin or solo piano at ambient conversation level, repertoire biased toward classical and jazz standards that most guests recognize without having to identify, and long-form pieces that don't force applause moments into a conversation-first evening. She takes cues from the host on volume and duration, comes in and leaves without disrupting service, and works cleanly around plated dinner arrivals.
