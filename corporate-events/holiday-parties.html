Live music for your corporate holiday party.

Solo violin for arrivals, jazz piano through seated dinner, and DJ + live violin for after-dinner dancing — one artist across the whole evening. NYC executive holiday parties, agency year-ends, hedge fund and family office holidays, brand celebrations.

What corporate holiday parties actually need from music.

The most-underrated element of a good corporate holiday party is that the music arc runs as one arc — not three separate DJ / band / string quartet bookings hoping to line up. A holiday party where the strings during cocktails, the jazz piano through dinner, and the reception DJ are all the same artist reads as intentional. The room shifts feel across the evening the way you actually want it to.

That is what this booking does. Julie holds all three formats and moves through them across a single evening with one load-in, one contact for the coordinator, and one contract instead of three. The savings on coordination usually outweigh the cost difference to hiring three vendors, and the room reads it.

Corporate holiday party logistics.

December is Julie's tightest calendar. Bookings for corporate holiday parties should come in by October at latest for weekend dates, and September if the event is on a Saturday in mid-December. Weeknight parties (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) hold longer into the season.

Julie signs event NDAs same-day, issues certificate of insurance within 48 hours naming the venue as additional insured, and works with the client's catering, AV, and production teams on run-of-show coordination. Custom holiday-song arrangements (a client company song, a brand jingle for a walk-on moment) are available with 4-6 weeks of lead time.
