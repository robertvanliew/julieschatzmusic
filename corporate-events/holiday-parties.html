Live Music for Corporate Holiday Parties in NYC and NJ

Julie Schatz plays live violin and jazz piano for corporate holiday parties in New York City and New Jersey, and can carry the night into dancing with a DJ and live violin set. One musician covers arrivals, dinner and the dance floor.

Corporate engagements begin at $3,000 and are quoted individually. December 2026 dates are open now.

How the night usually runs

Most company holiday parties have three parts, and each one needs something different from the music.

  • Arrivals: solo violin. People are coming in from the office, finding a drink and saying hello. Solo violin gives the room a welcome without getting in the way of conversation.
  • Dinner: jazz piano. Standards and soul at a level people can talk over. If there are remarks, a toast or a short awards segment, Julie plays people on and off and stays quiet while they speak.
  • Dancing: DJ and live violin. Once dinner is cleared the DJ takes over and the violin is played live over the set. Sound and lighting come with it.

You can book all three or only the part you need. When one musician carries the whole evening there is one contract, one load-in and one person for your planner to coordinate with, and the music moves from one part of the night to the next without a gap.

Formats

  • Solo violin. One musician, a small footprint, and the easiest fit for arrivals and cocktail hour.
  • Jazz piano. Standards, soul and modern covers at conversation level for dinner.
  • Violin and piano duo. Julie switching between the two, or the two-musician Soul Shades duo for a fuller sound.
  • DJ and live violin. A full DJ set with violin played live over it, for when the room is ready to dance.

If you only need a DJ, DJ DX handles those bookings. See holiday party DJ with DJ DX.

Holiday songs

Tell Julie which holiday songs you want when you inquire and she will confirm what fits the set. Up to three requests from the existing list are included with every booking, and the jazz standards, soul and pop on it work at a holiday party too. You can see the full 255-song repertoire.

December dates and lead time

December is a short month with a lot of parties in it. Reach out as soon as you have a date, even if the venue and headcount are not final. Julie replies to every inquiry within 24 hours.

Working with your procurement team

The paperwork is straightforward:

  • W-9 available on request.
  • Certificate of insurance available on request, with the venue named if the venue requires it.
  • Invoicing. The engagement is invoiced to the company.
  • House AV. She is comfortable plugging into a venue's in-house sound and working from a run of show with your production team.

What to send with your inquiry

The more Julie knows, the faster she can come back with a real number. The useful details are the date, the venue, the guest count, the start and end time, and which parts of the night you want music for. If the venue has in-house AV or you are working with a production company, mention that too. If there is a program, such as remarks, a toast or awards, a rough run of show helps her plan where the music goes.

Questions

How much does live music for a corporate holiday party cost?

Corporate engagements begin at $3,000 and are quoted individually based on the format, the number of hours, the guest count and any production needs. Send the details and Julie replies with a number within 24 hours.

Can one musician cover the whole party?

Yes. Julie plays solo violin for arrivals, jazz piano through dinner, and then performs live violin over a DJ set for dancing. That means one contract and one point of contact for the whole night. You can also book a single part of the evening.

How early should we book a December date?

As early as you can. December is a short month with a lot of parties in it, so reach out as soon as you have a date, even if the venue and headcount are not final.

Can you provide a W-9 and a certificate of insurance?

Yes. Both are available on request, and the certificate of insurance can name the venue when the venue requires it. The engagement is invoiced to the company.

Do you bring your own sound system?

Yes. A sound system scaled to the room is included, and the DJ and live violin format includes sound and lighting. At venues with in-house AV, Julie can plug into the house system and work with your production team.

Can we request specific songs?

Yes. Tell Julie the songs you want when you inquire, including any holiday songs, and she will confirm what fits the set. Requests from the 255-song repertoire are part of every booking.
