What activations need that a playlist can't deliver.

A brand activation is a temporary environment engineered to change how people feel about the brand for the next twenty minutes. Music has more leverage in that window than in almost any other event format because guests aren't warmed up — they're walking in cold, deciding in the first thirty seconds whether the space earns their attention. A live musician holding the room does that work; a Spotify playlist through overhead speakers cannot.

This booking is built for that specific job. Solo violin at the door as guests arrive, an original score composed to your brand film and performed live under the reveal, then a DJ + live violin transition into the social hour so the space stays warm without a hard cutover. Julie holds all three formats with one contract and one load-in.
