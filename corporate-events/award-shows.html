What separates a memorable ceremony from a stiff one.

The moment that reads best in a ceremony is when an honoree walks up to accept an award and something in the room shifts. Music does most of that lift. A custom walk-on theme composed for that person — even eight bars — reframes their walk from a program item into a real moment. Multiply across five or six honorees and the whole ceremony has a spine that carries the audience.

This booking builds that spine into the run of show. Julie composes short walk-on themes for each honoree based on your brief (industry, tenure, or a musical detail from the person), locks them in advance with your production team, and performs them live from the stage. Between honorees, transitions and stings under video segments. After the program, the same artist pivots into a DJ + live violin after-party.

How production works with your team.
