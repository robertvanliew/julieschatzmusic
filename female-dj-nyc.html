Booking a female DJ in New York

Female DJ inquiries in New York usually come from one of three directions: a bride or planner who wants specifically what the search says, a corporate coordinator building a diverse-vendor lineup for a brand event, or a private client throwing a party for a room where the DJ shouldn't be an afterthought. All three are welcome; the pricing above is the same across all of them.

Sets run four hours as standard and cover Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, Long Island, Westchester, Northern New Jersey, Connecticut, and — from mid-May through October — the Hamptons and North Fork.
